Questions

Things people ask first.

Can you really give injections?

Yes. The owner is a licensed veterinary technician and does this at the clinic every day. Insulin, B12, sub-Q fluids, post-op meds. Bring the protocol from your vet and we follow it exactly.

Who comes to my house?

One of the two of us, and you'll have met both. Medical visits are always the vet tech. Standard visits and overnights can be either of us.

How do you get in?

Lockbox, spare key or smart lock code, your choice. Keys are tagged with a number only, never your name or address.

What does an overnight include?

We're at your place from about 8 pm to 7 am, plus a midday visit and a dinner visit. Any medication during the stay is included. Two-night minimum on holidays.

How do I know the visit happened?

GPS check-in and check-out, a photo and a written report after every visit: what they ate, meds given and when, anything unusual. Overnights get a morning summary.

What's your cancellation policy?

How far ahead should I book?

A few days is plenty for visits. Overnights and holidays fill up, so give us a couple of weeks when you can. Inside 48 hours works if we have room, with a small last-minute fee.
