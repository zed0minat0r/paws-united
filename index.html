Questions

Questions
Who comes to my house?

One of the two of us, and you'll have met both at the meet & greet.

My pet takes medication. Is that okay?

Yes. Our owner is a licensed veterinary technician, so pills, insulin, injections and post-op care are all fine. Bring the routine from your vet and we follow it exactly.

What does an overnight include?

We're at your place from about 8 pm to 7 am, plus a midday visit and a dinner visit. Any medication during the stay is included. Two-night minimum on holidays.

How do I know the visit happened?

GPS check-in and check-out, a photo and a written report after every visit: what they ate, meds given and when, anything unusual. Overnights get a morning summary.
